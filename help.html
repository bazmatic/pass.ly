Pass Tracker — Help

Overview

Pass Tracker records the outcome of every pass during a match in real time. Tap the big buttons as each pass happens — the app tracks accuracy, streaks, goals, and timing so you can review performance after the game.

Your session is saved automatically. If you close or refresh the page, your data will still be there when you reopen it.

Starting a Match

Tap ▶ 1st to begin the first half. The match clock starts immediately.

▶ 1st 00:00
▶ 2nd

Use the HT button to mark the start and end of half time — this creates a visible break in the stats timeline.

When the second half begins, tap ▶ 2nd. You'll be asked to confirm — once started, first half data is locked. The clock continues from where it left off.

▶ 1st 34:12
▶ 2nd 00:00

Recording Passes

✓ Complete — pass was received successfully. Increments the streak counter.
✗ Failed — pass was intercepted or went astray. Resets the streak and counts against accuracy.
↺ Streak Reset Lost possession — the team lost the ball without a direct pass failure (e.g. a tackle or the ball went out). Resets the streak but does not count as a failed pass, so accuracy is unaffected.
The streak counts consecutive completed passes. It resets on any fail, possession loss, or goal. The best streak (⭐) records the highest run achieved in the session.

Recording Goals

⚽ FOR Goal for — your team scored. Resets the pass streak.
⚽ AGT Goal against — the opposition scored. Resets the pass streak.

The live score is shown in the header and updates as goals are recorded. Goals appear as diamond markers in the pass trail and on the stats timeline. They don't affect pass accuracy.

Utility Buttons

↩ Undo — removes the last recorded event (passes, goals, anything). Greyed out when there's nothing to undo.
↗ Share — sends a stats summary via the system share sheet. On desktop it copies to the clipboard instead.
⬇ Export — downloads the full session as a pass-tracker-YYYY-MM-DD.json file. Load this into the Stats Viewer for charts and analysis.
🗑 Reset — clears all data and restarts from zero. A confirmation prompt appears first. Cannot be undone.

Viewing Stats

After the match, tap ⬇ Export, then open the Stats Viewer and drop the file in. You'll see accuracy over time, a per-pass timeline with goal markers, and quarter-by-quarter breakdowns.
